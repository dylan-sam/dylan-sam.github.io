- [**Danganronpa Fanmade Animation**](https://youtu.be/Q2c7nJ6CtEg?si=uXsn30yXFjlRZn4t): This 3D animation was made in Blender back in 2018. The initial goal of my high school thesis was to make a 3D animation, but it turned out to be way more complex than I thought. Instead, as you can read above, I coded games instead, but I went back to my original project a few years later. I took inspiration from the 3D animations of a game called "Danganronpa" and made my own animation based on these. As of November 2026, the video has 179,000 views on YouTube! Again, this project uses proprietary material, so I'm not sharing the original files publicly.
