- [**Piano Practice Companion**](https://github.com/dylan-sam/piano-practice-companion): Piano Practice Companion is an open-source web & mobile app allowing you to practice your musical instrument with the help of backing tracks and lead sheets coming from the ChoCo database (which includes iReal Pro Forums). You can play a track, as well as practice chord progression ear training or sight reading using these same tracks. [Try the web demo here](https://dylan-sam.github.io/piano-practice-companion/).
